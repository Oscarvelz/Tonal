# TONAL — color foundation (research verdict, 2026-11-10)

The manuals TONAL's color data is founded on, chosen by Oscar's research
pass in the main chat. Methods and dimensions are encoded in the app's own
words; no book text, photos, or fan artwork is ever copied, and the app is
never marketed under the SciART™, 12 Blueprints™, or Color Me Beautiful™
names.

## The stack

1. **Palettes / backbone — the SciART 12-season system** (Kathryn Kalisz),
   via one fan lineage plus Christine Scaman's *Return to Your Natural
   Colours* (12 Blueprints). It is the only system matching the app's exact
   12 `PERSONAL_SEASONS` and its undertone × value × chroma model (Munsell
   H V/C — seasons as bounded HVC regions).
2. **Men's rules — Alan Flusser, *Dressing the Man* (2002).** Two laws:
   (a) the contrast in the clothes must mirror the contrast between the
   wearer's skin and hair/eyes; (b) repeat a skin or hair tone in a garment
   near the face to frame it. Pattern rule: like patterns must differ
   maximally in scale or they vibrate. (No belt-matches-shoe matrix exists
   in Flusser — never attribute it to him.)
3. **Harmony theory — Johannes Itten, *The Art of Color* (1961)**, via
   *The Elements of Color* (1970): 12-hue circle, seven contrasts (hue,
   light-dark, cold-warm, complementary, simultaneous, saturation,
   extension), harmony sets from complementary pairs, triads and tetrads,
   extension ratios (yellow:violet 1:3, orange:blue 1:2, red:green 1:1).
4. **Fame / onboarding — Carole Jackson, *Color Me Beautiful* (1980) and
   *Color for Men* (1985).** Too coarse to be the backbone; the front door
   only. Her garment-role split (suits / shirts / accessories / leisure)
   is the pattern `palettes.ts` already uses.

Supplement: David Zyla, *Color Your Style* (2010) — 8 body-extracted
"true colours" with fixed role semantics; legitimises the
dominant / secondary / accent role labels. No fixed list to transcribe.

Rejected: Suzanne Caygill (bespoke, no fixed list — credit only), Josef
Albers (colour relativity, no harmony scheme), Faber Birren (industrial;
Itten's editor), David Kibbe (shape/essence), John Molloy (audience
perception, dated).

## Hard constraints

- **No canonical 12-season palette exists as a free hex list.** The colours
  live only in paid fans: NDU Colors (78 named colours per season) or True
  Colour International (65+ per season, spectrophotometer-measured).
  Transcription = buy ONE lineage, measure every chip, store
  name + hex/Lab + HVC + season + neutral/accent role.
- **Pick TCI or NDU — never mix lineages.** Identical season names do not
  guarantee identical colours across lineages.
- **The `palettes.ts` re-founding waits on Oscar's purchase decision.**
  Nothing has been bought; do not buy anything.
- 60-30-10 and belt-matches-shoe are house heuristics in `rules.ts` —
  unattributed, never credited to the books above.
- Known gaps no source covers: modern casual / streetwear / sneakers, and
  diverse-skin-tone validation of transcribed palettes — test, don't assume.
